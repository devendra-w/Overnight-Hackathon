# Examination Schedule & Procedures

- End-semester exams are held in the **last two weeks** of each semester;
  the exact date-wise timetable is published on the Exam Cell notice board
  and portal 3 weeks before exams begin.
- Hall tickets are released **1 week before exams** on the Student Portal
  and require no-dues clearance from the Library and Finance Office.
- Revaluation requests must be submitted within **5 days** of result
  declaration, with a fee of ₹300 per subject, via the Exam Cell portal
  form.
- Backlog/supplementary exams are conducted approximately 6 weeks after
  regular results are declared; registration window is announced via
  email and the portal.
- Students missing an exam due to a medical emergency may apply for a
  **special exam** within 3 days of the missed exam, with supporting
  medical documentation, approved by the Controller of Examinations.

This document does not cover individual result queries or marks disputes —
those go through the Grievance Redressal Cell.
