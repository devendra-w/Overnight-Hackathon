## Clinic Timings
Aliases: what are clinic timings, clinic hours, when is the doctor available, campus clinic timings

**For everyone:** The campus clinic (Dr. Morepen) operates **24x7**, with medicines available on-site.

## Emergency Medical Support
Aliases: is emergency medical support available, medical emergency, ambulance, urgent medical help

**For everyone:** Yes — the clinic operates round the clock, so emergency medical support is available at any hour, not just during standard daytime hours.

**For freshers:** If you or someone near you needs urgent medical attention, go straight to the campus clinic regardless of the time — you don't need to wait for a "normal" time slot or get prior approval from anyone.
