# Student Leave & Attendance Policy

- Minimum attendance required to sit for end-semester exams: **75%** per
  course.
- Medical leave: submit a doctor's certificate to the Academic Office
  within 7 days of returning to class. Medical leave is capped at 10 days
  per semester for attendance condonation purposes.
- Attendance shortfall between 65–75% requires a **Condonation Request**,
  approved by the Head of Department, filed before the last teaching week.
- Below 65% attendance: no condonation possible; student must repeat the
  course.
- Leave of absence for a full semester (e.g. medical, personal emergency)
  requires approval from the Proctor, using VTOP under Hostel Approval section.
  Form available on the Student Portal.

Department-specific attendance rules (e.g. labs, clinical postings) may
differ — check with your department office for course-specific exceptions.
