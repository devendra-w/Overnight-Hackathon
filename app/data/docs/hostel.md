# Hostel & Accommodation Policy

## Room Allotment
Aliases: how do I get a room, room allotment, hostel booking, how is hostel assigned

**For freshers:** Hostel allotment is done automatically at the start of the academic year based on application priority and seat availability — you don't need to apply separately.

**For returning students:** Room-change requests open **2 weeks into the semester** via the Hostel Portal.

## Hostel In-Timings
Aliases: what are hostel timings, hostel curfew, what time to be back in hostel, hostel in-time

**For everyone:** Hostel in-time is **8:30 PM**, applicable to both boys' and girls' hostels.

**For freshers:** Plan evening activities (sports, events, outings) so you're back well before 8:30 PM, not exactly at the deadline.

## Mess Fees & Waivers
Aliases: mess fee, mess fee waiver, medical diet mess

**For everyone:** Mess fees are billed quarterly along with hostel fees. Mess fee waivers for medical dietary needs require a doctor's note submitted to the Warden's office.

## Guest Stays
Aliases: can guests stay in my room, family visiting hostel, guest house

**For everyone:** Guest stays are not permitted in student rooms. A separate guest house is available for visiting family, bookable via Administration.

## Reporting Maintenance Issues
Aliases: how to report maintenance, room maintenance issue, hostel complaint, broken fan, plumbing issue hostel

**For everyone:** Maintenance issues should be reported to the Warden's office for your block, or filed directly online at **https://snm.vitbhopal.ac.in/hc/en**.

Disciplinary matters and hostel fee disputes are handled directly by the Warden's office, not covered here.
