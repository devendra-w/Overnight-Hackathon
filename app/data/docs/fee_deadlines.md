# Fee Payment

Aliases: what are fee deadlines, when is fee due, fee payment date, how do I pay fees, fee payment process

**For freshers:** Your full-year fee is paid as a **one-time payment before the start of the academic year**, as part of onboarding — this happens before you join, not per semester like returning students.

**For returning students:**
- Odd semester (Aug–Dec) fees are due by **September 15**.
- Even semester (Jan–May) fees are due by **February 10**.
- A late fee of ₹500/week applies after the deadline, capped at ₹5,000.

**For everyone:** Fee payment is done online via **VTOP → Payments section**. You can also download your fee receipt from the same section, filtered by year or semester.

## Scholarships & Refunds

- Students on an approved scholarship must submit the scholarship letter to the Finance Office at least 10 working days before the deadline to avoid late charges.
- Fee refund requests (for withdrawal/transfer) must be filed within 30 days of payment, via the Refund Request Form on the portal.

## Fee Queries

For fee waivers, installment plans, disputes about the amount charged, or any other fee-related query, contact the Finance Office at **feescollection@vitbhopal.ac.in** — this document does not cover individual account adjustments.
