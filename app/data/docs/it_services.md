## Forgot Student Portal (VTOP) Password
Aliases: forgot vtop password, cant login to portal, reset student login, portal password recovery

**For everyone:** Use the "Forgot Password" option directly on the VTOP login page. If that doesn't resolve it, contact CTS at **cts@vitbhopal.ac.in**.

**For freshers:** Your initial VTOP credentials were issued during your Freshers Portal registration — this is a different login from any personal email account. Don't confuse the two.

## Connecting to Campus Wi-Fi
Aliases: how to connect to wifi, campus wifi setup, wifi id and password, connect to campus network

**For everyone:** The CTS team sends your Wi-Fi ID and password by email. When connecting for the first time, choose **TOFU (Trust On First Use)** when prompted to accept the certificate.

**Important:** Wi-Fi access is tied to your device's MAC address. If you switch devices or your Wi-Fi stops working, you'll likely need to register the new device's MAC address — contact CTS to sort this out.

## Wi-Fi Not Working / Troubleshooting
Aliases: wifi not working, cant connect to internet, wifi issue, internet not working, wifi problem

Try in order:
1. Confirm you're using the Wi-Fi ID/password sent by CTS via email (not your VTOP login).
2. Re-check the certificate was accepted via TOFU when you first connected.
3. If you've changed devices recently, this is very likely a MAC address registration issue.
4. If still unresolved, email **cts@vitbhopal.ac.in** directly with your device details and the issue.

**Common confusion:** Wi-Fi credentials and VTOP login credentials are two completely separate systems — mixing them up is the most common reason students think their "password isn't working."
