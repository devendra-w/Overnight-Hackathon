# Student ID Card & Campus Access

- New students receive their ID card during orientation week from the
  Administration Office.
- Lost/damaged ID card: file a report at the Administration Office with a
  ₹5000 reissue fee; a temporary paper gate pass is issued same-day while
  the new card is processed (3–5 working days).
- ID cards double as library access and hostel mess cards — report loss
  immediately to prevent misuse.
- Campus access after 9 PM (library, labs) requires a Late Access Pass,
  approved by the department coordinator, for academic work only.
- Visitors must be registered at the main gate; students are responsible
  for guests they bring on campus.

For hostel-specific access issues (room keys, biometric access), contact
Hostel Administration separately.
